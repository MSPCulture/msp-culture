# Return on Attendance

*Because SWAG doesn't count as ROI.*

A printable conference workbook for MSP leaders and their teams. It turns a trip to IT Nation, a vendor summit, or any industry show into a plan you can act on, then makes you decide whether it was worth it while the answer is still honest.

**Download:** [Return_on_Attendance_v1.0.pdf](Return_on_Attendance_v1.0.pdf) (12 pages, US Letter)

## What's in it

| When | Section | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Two weeks out | **01 Before You Go.** A team huddle to name your top 3 business problems and the one big problem you want to solve on this trip, plus a vendor game plan and five questions to ask every vendor | 03–04 |
| One week out | **02 Build Your Agenda.** Pick at least one session you love, one you need, and one you want, and list the people you want to meet | 05 |
| At the show | **03 At the Show.** Session notes, a vendor and people log, and an ideas parking lot | 06–08 |
| Wheels up | **04 The Flight Home.** Three takeaways, then three moves in the next 90 days for one of them | 09 |
| One week after | **05 The ROI Call.** Cost against measurable value, a gut-check scorecard out of 30, and a decision on next year | 10–11 |

## How to use it

1. Print it, or open it in a handwriting app (GoodNotes, Notability, reMarkable, and so on).
2. Do pages 03–05 with your team before you fly. Everything at the show should trace back to the problems you wrote down there.
3. Print extra copies of page 06 (session notes) for a multi-day show.
4. Fill in page 09 before the plane lands, and pages 10–11 one week after you're back. In six months you'll remember the party, not the sessions, so make the call now.

Printing a booklet: 12 pages folds into three sheets. Use your PDF reader's booklet print option and staple at the fold.
