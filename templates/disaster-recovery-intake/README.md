# Disaster Recovery Intake Workbook

A self-guided intake workbook that collects everything an organization needs before writing a disaster recovery plan, built for MSPs to hand to client IT teams.

**Get it free:** [toolbox.mspculture.com/l/dr-intake](https://toolbox.mspculture.com/l/dr-intake)

The workbook now lives in the MSP Culture toolbox, where you'll always get the latest version (currently v1.0.1).

## What's in it

- 13 tabs covering business impact, critical apps, data, infrastructure, network, backups, break-glass access, contacts, vendors, recovery sites and communications
- RTO and RPO set by department heads, then checked automatically against backup frequency and real restore times
- A Readiness Snapshot with 17 checks, like apps with no owner, backups with no immutable copy, and break-glass accounts nobody can reach when the identity provider is down
- Cyber insurance details, including the policy conditions that can void coverage
- White-label ready: enter the client's name once and it appears on every tab

Requires Microsoft Excel 2010 or later, or LibreOffice Calc. No macros, no external connections, no add-ins.
