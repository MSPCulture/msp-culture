# msp-culture
MSP Culture — public tools, templates, and resources for MSPs

## Assessments

| Assessment | What it measures | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| [Client Relationship Health](assessments/relationship-health/) | Governance, service experience, feedback, commercial health, strategic alignment, and churn risk triggers for each client relationship | v1.0 |

## Templates

| Template | What it's for | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| [Disaster Recovery Intake Workbook](templates/disaster-recovery-intake/) | Self-guided intake for client IT teams: business impact, critical apps, data owners, infrastructure, backups, break-glass access, contacts, vendors, and automatic gap checks before writing a DR plan | v1.0 |
| [Return on Attendance](templates/return-on-attendance/) | Printable conference workbook: team huddle and vendor plan before you go, love/need/want agenda, at-the-show notes, a 90-day plan on the flight home, and an ROI scorecard to decide on next year | v1.0 |

## Scripts

| Script | What it does | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| [Ticket Timezone Warning](scripts/ticket-timezone-warning/) | ConnectWise PSA userscript that warns techs when a ticket contact is outside business hours or on a holiday, in the contact's own timezone. No API keys, nothing leaves the browser | v1.3.1 |
