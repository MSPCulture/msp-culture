# Client Relationship Health Assessment

A quarterly, evidence-based read on the health of each client relationship, built for MSPs. It tells you which clients are drifting before they send the cancellation email.

**Get it free:** [toolbox.mspculture.com/l/relationship-health](https://toolbox.mspculture.com/l/relationship-health)

The assessment now lives in the MSP Culture toolbox, where you'll always get the latest version (currently v1.0.1).

> **Internal use only.** It rates the client's behavior and your relationship with them. Use it to prepare for the QBR; don't share it with the client.

## What's in it

- 29 items across governance, service experience, feedback, commercial health, strategic alignment and risk triggers
- 16 items that score themselves from your PSA, CSAT and accounting data; judgment items need evidence before they count
- Critical triggers that flag a client At Risk no matter the score, like champion turnover, an ownership change or an internal IT hire
- A health-versus-profitability matrix that recommends a play: Protect & Expand, Reprice, Rescue, or Fix or Exit
- A prioritized action list, editable thresholds and weights, and a spot for your logo

Run it quarterly about two weeks before each QBR, 120 days before renewal, and whenever the main contact changes.

Requires Microsoft Excel 2010 or later, or LibreOffice Calc. No macros, no external connections, no add-ins.
