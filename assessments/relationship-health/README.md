# Client Relationship Health Assessment

A quarterly, evidence-based read on the health of each client relationship, built for MSPs.

Technical assessments measure a client's environment. This one measures the relationship: governance, service experience, feedback, commercial health, strategic alignment, and known risk triggers. It tells you which clients are drifting before they send the cancellation email.

**Download:** [Relationship_Health_Assessment_v1.0.xlsx](Relationship_Health_Assessment_v1.0.xlsx) (or grab it from the [v1.0 release](https://github.com/MSPCulture/msp-culture/releases/tag/relationship-health-v1.0))

> **Internal use only.** This workbook rates the client's behavior and your relationship with them. Use it to prepare for the QBR. Do not share it with the client.

## What's in it

- **29 items across six dimensions:** Governance & Engagement, Service Experience, Feedback Loop, Commercial Health, Strategic Alignment, and Risk Triggers.
- **Mostly data-driven.** 16 items score automatically from figures in your PSA, CSAT tool, and accounting system, so the account owner isn't grading their own work. Judgment items require evidence before they're considered complete.
- **Critical items.** Champion turnover, escalation behavior, and risk triggers (ownership change, internal IT hire, competitor contact, budget cuts, unreviewed incidents) can force an At Risk rating regardless of score.
- **Health × Profitability.** Pairs the relationship rating with agreement margin to recommend a play: Protect & Expand, Reprice, Rescue, or Fix or Exit.
- **Prioritized action list** with a recommended play for every item that isn't Satisfactory.
- **Tunable.** Every threshold, weight, and rating band is editable on the Thresholds tab.
- **White-label ready.** Logo placeholder on the Start Here and Summary tabs.

## When to run it

- Quarterly, about two weeks before each QBR
- Within 30 days of a change in the client's primary contact or decision-maker
- 120 days before agreement renewal
- After a major outage or security incident

## Requirements

Microsoft Excel 2010 or later (also works in LibreOffice Calc). No macros, no external connections, no add-ins. Excel will open a downloaded copy in Protected View; click **Enable Editing** to fill it in.
